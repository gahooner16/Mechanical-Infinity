# Mechanical-Infinity
https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/mechanical-infinity

Welcome to Mechanical Infinity, a tech-focused Skyblock modpack inspired by Mechanical Mastery. Suspended in a void, players bypass traditional resource sifting by building complex automation lines with mods like Mekanism and Oritech. Progression centers on automating unique Essences, which unlock advanced technology tiers and drive your factory forward.
The endgame scales up dramatically through the Replication mod, which deliberately embraces tedious micro-crafting. By scanning materials and managing intricate crafting chains alongside massive power grids, players can digitally duplicate rare components and dense blocks. This emphasizes complex processing setups, shifting your focus entirely toward massive power infrastructure and grand-scale factory design.
